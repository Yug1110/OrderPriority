Shipping policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Where we deliver

We ship across India to all pincodes serviced by our courier partners. If your pincode isn't serviceable, we'll tell you when we confirm your order and offer a full refund of anything paid.

Dispatch and delivery time

  • Orders are packed and dispatched within working days of confirmation (and payment, for prepaid orders).
  • Delivery usually takes working days after dispatch, depending on your location.
  • Orders are not dispatched on Sundays or public holidays.

Shipping charges

  • Free shipping on orders of or more.
  • A flat fee of applies to smaller orders.

Cash on delivery (COD)

COD is available on orders of or more, with a handling fee of . Please keep the exact amount ready. We may ask for a small advance or switch an order to prepaid if a pincode or address has a history of refused deliveries.

Tracking

Once your order ships, we'll send the courier name and tracking link on WhatsApp, and the courier will send SMS updates.

Delays, wrong addresses and refused parcels

  • Courier delays caused by weather, strikes or other events outside our control can occasionally happen; we'll keep you updated.
  • Please double-check your address and phone number. If a parcel comes back because of an incorrect address or no one being available after multiple attempts, re-shipping will be charged.
  • Refusing a COD parcel without reason may make future orders prepaid-only.
