Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you use and how we use it, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Order details you give us: name, phone number, delivery address, pincode, the items you order and any notes. These reach us when you send your order on WhatsApp.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp or email.
  • Your bag is saved only in your own browser (local storage) so it's still there when you come back. It isn't sent to us until you place an order.

We don't collect card or bank details; payments are handled by our payment partner on their secure pages.

How we use it

  • To confirm, pack, ship and support your order, including exchanges and refunds.
  • To contact you about your order.
  • With your consent only, to send offers or new-arrival updates. You can opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

Only the partners needed to complete your order: our courier and shipping partner (name, phone, address), our payment partner (for payment links), and WhatsApp (Meta) as the messaging service. We never sell your data.

This website is hosted by Vercel and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services receive technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed for delivery, exchanges and our legal and tax obligations (usually up to 8 years for invoices), then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent for marketing, by writing to us. We'll respond within 30 days.
