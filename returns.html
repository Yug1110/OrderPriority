Returns, exchange & refunds

Last updated: 24 September 2026

We want every piece to fit and feel just right. Here's how exchanges, returns and refunds work.

Size exchange

  • Request a size exchange within days of delivery.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, with all tags attached and in the original packaging.
  • Exchanges depend on stock. If your size isn't available, you can choose another product or get a store credit.

Returns

For hygiene reasons, we don't accept returns for change of mind on baby clothing. Newborn accessories (caps, mittens, bibs) can't be exchanged unless they arrive damaged.

Damaged, defective or wrong item

  • Tell us within hours of delivery on WhatsApp, with your order ID, photos of the item and, ideally, an unboxing video.
  • We'll arrange a free pickup and send a replacement, or a full refund if a replacement isn't available.

How to request an exchange

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with your order ID and the size you need.
  2. We'll confirm and arrange a pickup from your address (or share a return address if your pincode isn't covered for pickup).
  3. Once we receive and check the item, we ship the new size.

Your first size exchange on an order is free. We'll share any pickup charge for further exchanges before arranging it.

Cancellations

You can cancel any time before your order is dispatched by messaging us. After dispatch, orders can't be cancelled, but you can request an exchange once delivered as above.

Refunds

  • Approved refunds are processed within working days.
  • Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method. For COD orders, we refund to a UPI ID or bank account you share with us.
  • Shipping and COD fees are refunded only when the problem was our mistake (damaged, defective or wrong item).
