Last updated: 24 September 2026

Every baby grows at their own pace, so go by weight and height rather than age. If your baby is between sizes, choose the bigger one; they'll grow into it.

How to measure

  • Height: lay your baby flat and measure from the top of the head to the heel.
  • Chest: measure around the fullest part of the chest, under the arms.

Winter suits and hooded jumpsuits are cut with a little extra room for layering. Still unsure? Message us with your baby's weight and height and we'll suggest a size.
